Lifelong Wellness

Exploring mindfulness, wellness, and personal growth through meditation

Part 1: Reflection Questions

Connection Between Courses

This Lifelong Wellness class was my first real exposure to mindfulness, meditation, and mental wellness practices. Before this course, I hadn't taken any classes related to psychology, mental health, or relaxation techniques. Everything I learned — from breathing exercises to guided meditation — was completely new to me. One connection I made was with my Math class, where I started using some of the meditation and breathing practices before studying for exams. Taking a few minutes to clear my mind and focus helped me feel calmer and more focused, which made a real difference in how I approached my math work. This showed me how mindfulness techniques can directly support academic success, not just personal well-being.

Reflection on Fitness and Wellness

Before taking this class, I mostly thought about fitness and wellness in terms of physical activity and nutrition. Now, after completing this course, I understand that mental and emotional wellness are just as important for lifelong health. Learning about meditation and practicing mindfulness exercises each week changed my perspective significantly. The Zoom sessions where we practiced breathing techniques and meditation were especially influential. Moving forward, I plan to keep using mindfulness techniques in my life to reduce stress and improve my overall well-being, treating mental health as an essential part of staying healthy, not just an extra.

Behavior Change Assignment

Part 3: Signature Assignment Reflection

I chose the Behavior Change assignment because it helped me see that making mindfulness a regular part of my life is a real process that takes planning and commitment. Since this was my first experience with meditation and mindfulness, the assignment made me realize that small, consistent efforts are key to making a lasting habit. It showed me that moving from thinking about change to actually taking action requires clear goals and accountability. This really resonated with me because it connected the idea of personal growth with practical steps I could follow. In the future, I’ll use the approach I learned here to build other healthy habits in my life, both for mental wellness and beyond.
