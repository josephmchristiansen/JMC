COMM1560 - Radio Production

Exploring media, voice, and creativity in radio production

General Reflection

Taking this radio production class helped me see how much communication matters, even outside the studio. Learning how to write scripts, speak clearly, and connect with an audience taught me skills I now use in other areas of life—like presenting in other classes or talking more confidently in public. The hands-on practice made me better at thinking on my feet and expressing ideas effectively. One of the most valuable parts was learning how to pace myself during a live show. It taught me how to keep things flowing and avoid awkward silences. I also started recognizing how good communication makes people pay attention. These are lessons I’ll use in any career I pursue.

What's Happening Promo

This promo was meant to quickly grab attention and highlight a local event. I focused on using upbeat energy and concise writing to keep it engaging. One challenge was fitting everything into 30 seconds without rushing. I learned how to cut down words while keeping the message exciting and clear. It really showed me how much impact tone and pacing can have.

Remote Break

The goal of this break was to deliver casual but informative commentary in a live setting. Planning what to say while leaving room to react on the fly was the hardest part. This assignment helped me get more comfortable thinking out loud and keeping my voice confident, even if I stumbled. It was great practice for unscripted communication.

30-Second Commercial

This commercial needed to be punchy and memorable. I worked hard on writing a strong hook and choosing sound effects that matched the tone. It showed me how much planning goes into short-form advertising. I learned how to use music and pacing to create a mood that fits the product and makes the ad stick in your head.

Newscast

This assignment helped me practice clear delivery while sounding natural. Writing the script taught me how to pull key facts and stay objective, while reading it on-air helped me work on flow and pronunciation. It showed me how important clarity and pacing are when you're trying to sound professional and trustworthy.

Best of Air Check

This was a highlight reel of my best radio moments. It captured everything from transitions to storytelling. I used it to show what I’d learned across the course. Putting it together helped me see how much I’d improved in energy, timing, and audience engagement. It made me proud of my growth and made me want to keep going with radio and public speaking.
